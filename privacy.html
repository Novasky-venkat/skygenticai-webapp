Privacy Policy

Skygentic is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it and your choices when using our website, AI solutions and services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company or organization name
  • Contact information
  • Account information
  • Information submitted through forms or support requests
  • Information provided while using our AI agents and services

We may also automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

  • IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Pages visited
  • Usage activity
  • Log and diagnostic information
  • Cookies and similar technologies

Information Processed Through Our AI Services

Skygentic provides AI agents, workflow automation, orchestration and related enterprise AI services.

When customers use these services, information may be submitted to or processed by our systems to perform the requested tasks.

Depending on how a customer configures the service, this may include business information, documents, workflow data, prompts, instructions, system outputs and information received through connected third-party applications.

Customers are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights and permissions to provide information to Skygentic for processing.

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate Skygentic services
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Process AI agent and automation requests
  • Improve the performance and reliability of our services
  • Provide customer support
  • Monitor security and prevent misuse
  • Communicate service updates
  • Understand how our website and services are used
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information.

AI-Generated Content

Our services may use artificial intelligence to process information and generate responses, recommendations, workflows or other outputs.

AI-generated outputs may occasionally be incomplete, inaccurate or unexpected. Users should review important outputs before relying on them for business, financial, legal, medical or other significant decisions.

Third-Party Services and Integrations

Skygentic may integrate with third-party applications, APIs, AI models, cloud platforms and business systems.

When you connect a third-party service to Skygentic, certain information may be exchanged with that provider as necessary to perform the requested workflow.

Those third parties operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, understand usage and improve the user experience.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

How We Share Information

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate our website and services, such as hosting, cloud infrastructure, analytics, security, communications and technology providers.

We may also disclose information when required by law, regulation, court order or another valid legal process.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, meet business requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and comply with applicable laws.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the services being used.

Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to request access, correction or deletion of certain information.

You may contact us to submit a privacy-related request.

Children's Privacy

Skygentic services are intended for businesses and professional users and are not designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as our services, technologies or legal requirements change.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Skygentic handles information, please contact us through the contact information provided on the Skygentic website.
