Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

What is Agentic AI and how does it differ from traditional chatbots?

−

Agentic AI autonomously reasons, utilizes external tools, queries knowledge bases dynamically, and executes multi-step business transactions without manual static flowcharts.

Which messaging channels are supported out of the box?

+

WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, LINE, KakaoTalk, web widgets, and voice endpoints are all natively supported.

How does the design system enforce brand consistency?

+

Through atomic CSS custom properties in tokens.css, strict typography scales in typography.css, and Antigravity Agent guidelines in .gemini/GEMINI.md.
